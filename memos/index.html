Inlane (Lane)

Recommendation: Invest
01

Company name

Inlane Technologies, trading as Lane (inlane.in). Bengaluru. Pre-seed, ₹8.5 Cr raised in August 2026 from Kae Capital (lead), DeVC, Antler India and Panthera Peak [Ascendants, 12 Aug 2026].

02

Sector

Intra-city road passenger mobility, enabling technology: driver training and driver-behaviour data.

03

Introduction

Lane teaches people to drive in Bengaluru and fits sensors in its cars that turn every lesson into a driver-behaviour profile. The core idea is that the licence is not the finish line: 70% of its learners already hold a licence when they enrol [Ascendants].

India has about 25 crore licence holders [Ascendants, company figure] and 1.89 lakh road deaths a year [MoRTH], and fleets of every type need trained drivers. Most Indians learn from family or a local school that teaches to the test. Lane replaces that with vetted instructors scored on 15 parameters and a data record that insurers and fleets can price.

Founded by Samiksha Agarwal (ex-Antler, 3one4 Capital and EY; Shri Ram College of Commerce).

04

Recommendation: Invest

Invest in the next round. Lane has the largest disclosed learner base of any tech-led driving school we found, and a data layer that can grow beyond lessons.

ZeroMoblt

Recommendation: Invest
01

Company name

ZeroMoblt (zeromoblt.com). Hyderabad, founded April 2024. Pre-seed, ₹1.5 Cr in February 2026 co-led by Sandeep Varaganti (JioMart CEO), Ashok Agrawal and HNIs [Entrackr, 24 Feb 2026].

02

Sector

Intra-city road passenger mobility: school and college student transport, sold as a subscription.

03

Introduction

ZeroMoblt sells parents and institutions a monthly transport subscription with tracking and compliance, run on 3,000+ partner vehicles [Entrackr]. The core idea is to turn the unregulated school van into a tracked, compliant, prepaid service.

Student transport is worth ₹27,000 Cr a year: 1.5 crore students x ₹1,800 a month x 10 months. Parents pay today, but to vans with no tracking, no driver checks and no backup; ZeroMoblt adds all 3 behind one subscription.

Founded by Vijay Ganagam (CEO) and Alfred Lazarus [Entrackr], with Abhinav Dantuluri listed as co-founder [Crunchbase].

04

Recommendation: Invest

Invest in the seed round, expected in the first half of 2027. It is the only funded player in a ₹27,000 Cr gap and scores 20 of 25 on our seed-readiness scorecard.

Kyra Rides

Recommendation: Invest
01

Company name

Kyra Rides (kyrarides.in). Bengaluru and Mysuru. No institutional funding; ₹9 lakh lifetime spend [company data]. Pre-revenue, with 125 rides in its January pilot. Raising ₹8 Cr, mostly for Bengaluru driver acquisition and city expansion [founder].

02

Sector

Intra-city road passenger mobility: women-only ride-hailing.

03

Introduction

Kyra is a ride-booking app for women in Bengaluru, matched only with verified women drivers, with KYC-reviewed drivers and in-ride SOS [kyrarides.in]. The core idea is that the trip, not the job, keeps many urban women out of work.

Women-safe commute is worth ₹7,000 Cr a year inside a ₹28,000 Cr women's commute spend pool. 91% of women do not feel very safe on public transport in their city [Ola Mobility Institute 2019]; Kyra fixes the trip by controlling who drives.

Founded by Divyashri Patil and Shivansh Pahwa, both at Mesa School of Business; Shivansh is also at IIM Bangalore.

04

Recommendation: Invest

Invest at pre-seed, sized to the traction rather than the full ₹8 Cr ask. Kyra has 2,769 riders and 526 women drivers onboarded on ₹9 lakh of spend [company data], which is cheap demand. Only 57 drivers are active, so the first cheque should buy proof of liquidity in Bengaluru, with the rest of the ₹8 Cr tied to that milestone.
