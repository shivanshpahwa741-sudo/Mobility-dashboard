How India moves within its cities

Indians make 165.5 crore trips (>1 km) a day. Indian passengers spend ₹7.2 lakh crore a year, or ₹1,970 crore a day, on intra-city road travel in fares, fuel, maintenance, insurance and other running costs, as on Nov 2026.

    Factors influencing the intra-city mobility sector

    4 factors

    Drivers keep 80% of every app fare; surge is capped at 2x

    The Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines 2025, in force from 1 Jul 2025, set the driver share at 80% of the fare on a driver-owned vehicle and 60% on an aggregator-owned vehicle. Surge is capped at 2x the base fare and discounts at 50% below it, with the base fare set by each state. Drivers get ₹5 lakh of health cover and ₹10 lakh of term insurance, and vehicles may be at most 8 years old.

    PM E-DRIVE has an outlay of ₹10,900 crore. PM e-Bus Sewa funds 10,000 e-buses in 169 cities, and its ₹3,435 crore payment guarantee covers 38,000+ e-buses from FY25 to FY29.

    Source: MoRTH MVAG 2025; MHI PM E-DRIVE (PIB); MoHUA PM e-Bus Sewa; Union Cabinet.

    India runs 9.7 state buses per lakh people against a benchmark of 60

    58 state transport undertakings run 1,40,100 buses for 145 crore people, 84% below the benchmark of 60 buses per lakh. Coverage ranges from 5 buses per 10 lakh people in Bihar to 417 in Delhi. India has 1,46,560 km of national highway.

    Road crashes will kill 1.89 lakh people in 2026, after 1.83 lakh in 2025 and 1,77,175 in 2024. Two-wheeler riders are 46.2% of deaths, pedestrians 20.6% and car occupants 12.4%.

    Source: MoRTH SRTU review; MoRTH Road Accidents in India 2024; MoRTH year-end review; MoSPI NHTS FY26.

    61% of three-wheelers sold in FY26 were electric

    India registered 3.08 crore new vehicles in FY26. 61% of three-wheeler sales were electric, up from 57% in FY25, and 24% ran on CNG. 6.5% of two-wheeler sales were electric.

    App booking is the second shift. Ride-hailing apps carry 303 crore rides a year worth ₹38,500 crore, and 78% of those rides are on bikes and autos. 98% of auto trips are still booked offline.

    Source: VAHAN via IESA and Autocar Pro; FADA; MCA filings.

    53.7 crore Indians, 37% of the population, live in cities

    145 crore people live in India in 2026, and 37% of them live in cities. 64.1% of urban workers travel more than 1 km to work, against 54.5% in rural India. 68.1% of urban students travel more than 1 km to school or college.

    An urban Indian spends ₹694 a month on conveyance. 61.9% of working men travel more than 1 km to work, against 41.9% of working women.

    Source: MoSPI NSO NHTS FY26; MoSPI HCES; UN World Population Prospects.
