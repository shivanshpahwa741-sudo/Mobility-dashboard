SECTOR THESIS · NOVEMBER 2026

How India Moves

A sector thesis on how India moves within its city borders, covering only road transportation as the subsegment of the sector.

Executive summary

Indians make 165.5 crore trips (>1 km) a day, and 99%+ of them stay within a city. Intra-city road travel is the largest pool of passenger spend in India. Most of it runs through privately owned vehicles and informal fares, because public transport reaches too few people.

Step 1Road takes 85.4% of passenger spend, and intra-city road takes 82.8%

This thesis covers passenger mobility, which is every trip a person makes by choice. Indian passengers spend ₹8.7 lakh crore a year (2026) on conveyance across all modes and distances. Road takes ₹7.43 lakh crore. Intra-city road takes ₹7.2 lakh crore, which is ₹1,970 crore a day.

Urban residents spend ₹694 per person a month on conveyance. Rural residents spend ₹367.

99%+ of trips stay within the city

The National Household Travel Survey (NHTS) 2025-26 counts a trip as a movement of 1 km or more, with onward and return as 2 trips. It sampled 4,92,023 households. Only 11.3% of households make an overnight trip of 50 km or more in 28 days. Those trips add less than 0.1 crore trips a day against 165.5 crore.

Road, including walking and cycling, carries 94.4% of urban work trips. Metro, suburban rail and tram carry 5.0%.

Step 2Privately owned vehicles take 58.1% of intra-city road spend

Most people do not use public transport. Among urban workers travelling more than 1 km:

  • 58.0% use a privately owned vehicle (two-wheeler or car)
  • 8.8% use shared autos
  • 14.5% use public transport: 9.2% buses and 5.0% metro
  • 19.5% walk or cycle

State bus undertakings lose ₹35,000 crore a year because each bus-km costs ₹86 and earns ₹55. Thus, the lack of public transport connectivity in India is filled by privately owned and shared vehicles.

By vehicle

Across all trips, two-wheelers carry 33.6%, walking 30.0%, buses 11.9%, bicycles 11.7%, autos and e-rickshaws 7.3%, cars 2.5%, metro and rail 2.0% and other vehicles 1.0%. Three-wheelers are 45 lakh CNG or petrol autos and 25.3 lakh e-rickshaws. Other vehicles such as jugaads and chhakdas carry 70 lakh work trips a day.

Fares: offline autos collect 55% of ₹3.02 lakh crore

Autos and e-rickshaws make 150 crore trips a month, and app auto rides are 3 crore a month. That means 147 crore auto trips a month are still booked offline.

Ridership fell from 617 lakh a day in FY20 to 245 lakh in FY21 and has climbed back to about 600 lakh in FY26, lifted by free travel for women in Karnataka, Telangana, Delhi, Punjab and Tamil Nadu. Losses rose 72% over the same years. More riders have not closed the gap, because each bus-km still costs ₹31 more than it earns.

State buses carry 6.0 crore passengers a day on a fleet of 1,40,100. Each bus has 4.39 staff. Delhi city buses alone lose ₹10,950 crore a year.

Ride-hailing: every large platform loses money

App ride-hailing is ₹38,500 crore of fares a year from 303 crore rides. Bikes and autos are 78% of rides, but cabs collect 53% of fares because a cab ride costs more.

Customers pay

Size

Closest
MoveInSync: employer commute software and operations, ₹438 crore revenue in FY24. Routematic: managed employee transport, $40M Series C. DriverShaab: supplies trained drivers to employee transport operators, ₹2.82 crore pre-Series A. The leaders are past early stage.

2Driver EV financing, leasing and battery-as-a-service

Unmet need
70 lakh auto and e-rickshaw drivers have no credit history. Loans for electric three-wheelers cost 14% to 16%.
Customers pay
Drivers already pay daily rent of ₹300 to ₹500 for fleet electric three-wheelers and two-wheelers. Battery-as-a-service cuts the upfront cost of an electric three-wheeler by 35% to 40%.
Size
₹20,000 crore of electric three-wheeler loans and ₹10,800 crore of electric two-wheeler loans a year
Why now
Electric passenger three-wheelers went from 12.6% of sales in FY24 to 33.7% in FY26. Telematics lets lenders immobilise a vehicle.
Closest
Vidyut: battery-as-a-service financing, $16.5M raised. Chargeup: battery swaps and credit for e-rickshaw drivers, ₹22 crore in January 2026, 10,000+ drivers. PointO: lithium batteries and finance for e-rickshaw owners, ₹6.2 crore seed. Mufin Green: listed lender to EV buyers.

3Subscription transport for school and college students

Unmet need
1.5 crore students use school transport, much of it unregulated vans. 33.7% of urban students go by bus and 14.4% by auto.
Customers pay
Parents pay ₹1,500 to ₹4,000 a month in metros. ZeroMoblt has run 3.4 lakh student trips for 15+ institutions.
Size
₹27,000 crore a year
Why now
GPS tracking is now mandated on public service vehicles, and UPI autopay makes a monthly subscription easy to collect
Closest
ZeroMoblt: subscription school and college transport in Hyderabad, ₹1.5 crore pre-seed, 3.4 lakh trips. Legacy school bus contractors: local, unfunded and mostly offline.

4Feeders to metro stations

Unmet need
Metro grew from 248 km in 2014 to 1,095 km in December 2025, but stations sit beyond walking distance for many riders.
Customers pay
Riders already pay ₹10 to ₹30 a leg for shared autos and e-rickshaws. Yulu raised a $93M Series C in August 2026.
Size
₹8,800 crore a year
Closest
Yulu: short-trip electric two-wheeler rentals, $93M Series C. Namma Yatri: zero-commission auto and cab app with Bengaluru metro tickets, ₹39.75 crore pre-Series A extension in March 2026. MyPickup: subscription electric autos for housing societies, ₹1.5 crore seed.

5Safe commute for women

Unmet need
91% of women do not rate public transport very safe. Urban women join the labour force at 25.0%, against about 75% for urban men, and 41.9% of women workers travel more than 1 km to work, against 61.9% of men. The trip, more than the job, holds them back.
Customers pay
Fery Rides has run 65,000+ rides with 250+ women drivers. Women's share of Delhi bus riders rose from 33% to 42% after fares were made free, and 150 crore+ free pink tickets were issued by November 2024.
Size
₹7,000 crore a year. 1.3 crore urban working women ride paid modes and spend ₹1,500 a month, a ₹28,000 crore spend pool. 20% switching to a verified women-safe service at ₹2,250 a month gives ₹7,000 crore.
Closest
Fery Rides: women drivers on electric bike taxis and cabs for women riders, ₹2.075 crore seed. Sakhi: women-only autos in Bengaluru, unfunded pilot. MoveInSync and Routematic: employer-paid night-shift transport for women staff.

Step 6The best bets sit where customers already pay and only seed companies compete

Driver EV financing and student transport combine the largest early-stage pools with paying customers today. In student transport, ZeroMoblt is the only funded company and has raised ₹1.5 crore. In EV fleets for ride-hailing, the leading seed fleets have raised $5M or less each. Employee commute is larger at ₹72,000 crore a year, but its leaders are already at Series B and C.

Investment memos 1 to 3

Recommendation: Invest in Inlane (driver training and driver-behaviour data), ZeroMoblt (student transport, ₹27,000 crore a year) and Kyra Rides (women-safe commute, ₹7,000 crore a year). Read the 3 memos.

Step 7Risks

  • Regulation. Drivers keep at least 80% of each fare and surge is capped at 2x base, which limits platform take rates. Bike-taxi legality still varies by state.
  • State payment. The ₹3,435 crore Payment Security Mechanism covers 38,000+ e-buses. Contracts outside it depend on state undertakings that lose ₹35,000 crore a year.
  • Platform economics. The 4 ride-hailing platforms lost ₹2,483 crore in FY25. BluSmart shut in April 2025 after raising $142M.
  • Battery value. EVs are 61% of all three-wheeler sales, and fleet and lending economics rest on battery life and resale.
  • Data. No census has counted the population since 2011, and the National Household Travel Survey does not publish an all-trip mode share.
