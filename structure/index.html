    The forces that shape all of it

    Electric is cheaper per km in every class

    Over five years an e-auto costs ₹3.29 a km against ₹5.51 for CNG, which leaves its driver ₹8,965 a month better off. An EV cab costs ₹6.00 a km against ₹7.58.

    The open risk is the battery. Nobody knows what it is worth after five Indian summers. Lenders who track the vehicle remotely keep bad loans at 1.5 to 2.5%; the rest see 3.5 to 5.5%.

    Source: PPAC and state discom tariffs (Nov 2026); OEM and battery-as-a-service pricing; RevFin and Mufin Green Finance FY26.

    $1.15 billion in, half of it into ride-hailing

    Outside investors put $1.15 billion into Indian passenger mobility from FY23 to November 2026 across 86 deals. Uber's parent separately put in $330 million.

    Rapido is valued at $3 billion and Namma Yatri at ₹500 crore. Ola has been marked down from $7.3 billion to about $1.5 billion. Listed booking platforms trade far above operators: ixigo at 66x EBITDA and MakeMyTrip at 31x, against 17x for IndiGo.

    Source: Tracxn, Venture Intelligence and MCA share-allotment filings; NSE, BSE and NASDAQ filings, Nov 2026.

    The rules are still being written

    National rules now cap what platforms can take from drivers and how far fares can surge, but most states haven't enforced them. Bike taxis are legal state by state, and gig welfare laws are adding a cost that daily-pass apps mostly escape.

    For buses, the important rule is about payment. A central fund guarantees what states owe on e-bus contracts. Diesel and CNG contracts have no such guarantee.

    Source: MoRTH MVAG 2025; Maharashtra Bike-Taxi Rules 2025; Karnataka and Telangana gig worker acts; MHI PM E-DRIVE and PM e-Bus Sewa PSM guidelines.

    What has changed, and what it does

    Key rules affecting passenger mobility
    • Jul 2025 · Aggregator GuidelinesDrivers keep at least 80% of the fare and surge is capped at 2x. Most states haven't enforced them.
    • 2025 · Bike taxisMaharashtra allows electric bike taxis only. Karnataka's are in court, and Tamil Nadu has no rules yet.
    • 2025 to 2026 · Gig worker lawsKarnataka and Telangana passed platform worker acts. Five states levy 1 to 5% for gig welfare.
    • PM e-Bus Sewa payment fundGuarantees state payments on 38,000+ e-buses. Other contracts are paid 2 to 6 months late.
    • PM E-DRIVESubsidises electric commercial vehicles. Private e-cars get no central subsidy.

    Women ride a different system

    Only 42% of working women travel more than a kilometre to work, against 62% of men, and they spend 36% less doing it. Women hold under 18% of two-wheeler licences, so they walk, take buses and take autos far more.

    Salaried women use cabs more than men, mostly after 8 PM. In Indian metros they pay about 21% more to cover the same distance.

    Source: MoSPI / NSO NHTS FY26; Ola Mobility Institute (2019); WRI India (2026).

    Two-wheelers carry a third of trips and half the deaths

    Road crashes killed 1.77 lakh people in 2024 and 1.83 lakh in 2025. Nearly half were on two-wheelers and a fifth were walking.

    Buses carry 11% of trips and 2% of deaths, and the metro almost none. Every shift from bus to scooter makes the system more dangerous.

    Source: MoRTH, Road Accidents in India 2024 (Jun 2026); MoSPI NHTS FY26.
