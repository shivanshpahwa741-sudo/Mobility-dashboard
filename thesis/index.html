Step 1Intra-city road is ₹7.2 lakh crore of ₹8.7 lakh crore in passenger spend

Passenger mobility is every trip a person makes by choice. Goods and freight are outside this thesis. Indian passengers spend ₹8.7 lakh crore a year (2026) on conveyance across all modes and distances. Road takes ₹7.43 lakh crore of this, and intra-city road takes ₹7.2 lakh crore a year, which is ₹1,970 crore a day. Spend here means fares, fuel, maintenance, insurance and other running costs. Vehicle purchase and financing are excluded.

99%+ of trips stay within the city

The National Household Travel Survey (NHTS) 2025-26 counts a trip as a movement of 1 km or more, with onward and return as 2 trips. It sampled 4,92,023 households and 18,44,179 persons aged 6+. Indians make 165.5 crore such trips a day.

Step 2Privately owned vehicles carry 66.8% of urban work trips

Among urban workers travelling more than 1 km, 58.0% use personal vehicles and 8.8% use shared autos. 14.5% use public transport, of which buses carry 9.2% and metro 5.0%. State bus undertakings lose ₹35,000 crore a year (FY26): each bus-km costs ₹86 and earns ₹55. Thus, the lack of public transport connectivity in India is filled by private and shared vehicles.

1. 98% of auto trips are booked offline

Autos and e-rickshaws collect ₹460 crore a day. They make 150 crore trips a month, and app auto rides are 3 crore a month. That means 147 crore auto trips a month are still booked offline.

Step 7Risks

  • Regulation. MVAG 2025 fixes the driver share at 80% of fare and caps surge at 2x base fare, which limits platform take rates.
  • State payment. The ₹3,435 crore Payment Security Mechanism covers 38,000+ e-buses. Contracts outside it depend on state undertakings that lose ₹35,000 crore a year (FY26).
  • Platform economics. The 3 largest ride-hailing operators lost ₹2,321.4 crore in FY25. BluSmart shut down in April 2025 after raising $142M.
  • Battery value. EVs are 61% of 3W sales (FY26), and fleet economics rest on battery life.
  • Road deaths. 1.89 lakh road deaths a year are an all-road count, and the intra-city share is not published.
  • Data. No census has counted the population since 2011.
