How India moves within its cities

Indians make 165.5 crore trips (>1 km) a day. Indian passengers spend ₹7.2 lakh crore a year, or ₹1,970 crore a day, on intra-city road travel in fares, fuel, maintenance, insurance and other running costs, as on Nov 2026.

    Factors influencing the intra-city mobility sector

    4 factors

    Bike taxis are legal in some states and banned or unclear in others

    The Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines 2025, in force from 1 Jul 2025, let privately owned bikes run as taxis if the state agrees. Drivers keep 80% of the fare on their own vehicle, and surge is capped at 2x the base fare.

    Each state decides. Goa, Maharashtra (e-bikes only), Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan and West Bengal permit bike taxis. Karnataka banned them in 2025; the High Court lifted the ban in 2026 and the policy is still pending. Maharashtra's draft adds a levy of ₹5 a ride plus 2% of fares.

    PM e-Bus Sewa sanctioned 10,000 e-buses for 116 cities with ₹57,613 crore. By Mar 2026 only 4 cities were running them.

    Source: Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines 2025; state transport notifications; MoHUA PM e-Bus Sewa; MHI PM E-DRIVE.

    Metro has grown 4 times since 2014; city roads have slowed

    India ran 248 km of metro in 2014 and about 1,095 km in Dec 2025, across 26 cities. Roads have not kept up. Bengaluru's peak speed fell to 16.6 km/h in 2025, the 2nd slowest in the world, and 8 Indian cities are in the global top 50 for congestion.

    58 state transport undertakings run 1,40,100 buses for 145 crore people, or 9.7 buses per lakh against a benchmark of 60. Road crashes killed 1,77,175 people in 2024, and two-wheeler riders were 46.2% of them.

    Source: PIB; TomTom Traffic Index 2025; MoRTH Review of the Performance of State Road Transport Undertakings; MoRTH Road Accidents in India 2024.

    1 in 3 passenger autos sold in FY26 was electric

    Electric share of passenger auto sales rose from 12.6% in FY24 to 33.7% in FY26. Electric share of two-wheeler sales reached 6.6%. Including e-rickshaws, 61% of three-wheelers sold in FY26 were electric.

    Payments moved first. UPI handled 241.6 billion transactions in FY26, up from about 46 billion in FY22, and has 55.5 crore users.

    Booking is still offline. Ride-hailing apps carry 303 crore rides a year, worth ₹38,500 crore. Bikes and autos are 78% of those rides. Cabs are about 53% of the fares, because a cab ride costs about ₹330 and a bike ride about ₹60. 98% of auto trips are still booked offline.

    Source: VAHAN via EVreporter and Autocar Pro; FADA; NPCI; MCA filings.

    53.7 crore Indians, 37% of the population, live in cities

    Urban India grew from 37.7 crore people in 2011 to 53.7 crore in 2026 and will reach 59.4 crore by 2036. 64.1% of urban workers travel more than 1 km to work.

    Women travel less. 61.9% of working men travel more than 1 km to work, against 41.9% of working women. 91% of women do not rate public transport very safe, and urban female labour force participation is 25.0%.

    Source: MoSPI NSO NHTS FY26; NCP Technical Group 2019; MoSPI PLFS Apr 2026; Ola Mobility Institute.
